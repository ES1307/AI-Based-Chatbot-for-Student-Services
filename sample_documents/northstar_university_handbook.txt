NORTHSTAR UNIVERSITY - STUDENT SERVICES HANDBOOK (SAMPLE)

Academic registration and attendance
Students register for classes through the Student Portal during their assigned registration window. Before registering, students should consult their academic advisor and clear any holds. A student should normally maintain at least 75 percent attendance in each enrolled course. Students with exceptional circumstances should contact their course instructor and Academic Advising as early as possible.

Financial support
The Financial Aid Office provides guidance on scholarships, need-based grants, emergency assistance, and installment payment plans. Students seeking financial assistance should submit the Financial Support Form through the Student Portal and attach requested income or enrollment documents. The office aims to respond within five working days. For payment-plan questions, students may contact the Student Accounts desk.

Wellbeing and urgent support
The Student Wellbeing Centre offers confidential counselling, stress-management workshops, and referrals to external providers. Students may book an appointment through the wellbeing page or visit the centre for weekday drop-in hours from 1 PM to 3 PM. For an immediate safety concern, students should contact local emergency services or Campus Security. The chatbot cannot provide crisis counselling or emergency advice.

Campus services
The Library is open Monday to Friday from 8 AM to 9 PM and Saturday from 10 AM to 4 PM during term time. The Accessibility Office provides academic accommodations and assistive-technology consultations. Housing Services supports residents with room assignments, maintenance requests, and residence-life concerns. Contact details and current office hours are published in the Student Portal.

Scholarships
Scholarship applications open on 1 March and close on 30 April each academic year. Eligibility may depend on academic performance, financial need, community engagement, or programme of study. Applicants must review the individual scholarship criteria before submitting an application.
